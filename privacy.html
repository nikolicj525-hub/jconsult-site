Your rights

You can ask us at any time to:

  • see the personal data we hold about you and get a copy
  • correct data that is wrong or incomplete
  • delete your data, where we have no legal reason to keep it
  • restrict or object to how we use it
  • receive your data in a portable format
  • withdraw your consent, for example by using the unsubscribe link in any email

Email jovan@jovanconsult.com. We reply within one month.

Complaints

You can complain to Serbia's Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection (poverenik.rs). If you live in the EU, you can also complain to the data protection authority in your country.

Children

Our services are for businesses and organisations. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.
